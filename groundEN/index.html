This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Ground Ace 1944 (the "Game"), published by OurPuppy ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The Game is a free, single-player action game for Android that is supported by advertising and optional in-app purchases.

1. Summary

  • The Game does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details.
  • You can delete all local data by uninstalling the Game, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, and ask us to delete any data by email. See Data Deletion.

4. Advertising and your choices

On first launch the Game asks for your consent to ads. You may choose "No personalized ads"; the Game will still show ads, but we pass a "no consent" signal to our ad partners so they do not use your data for personalized advertising.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time: Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Purchasing "Remove Ads" disables interstitial and banner ads; rewarded ads remain available only when you choose to watch them.

5. Data retention

Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its storage. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies (typically up to 13 months for ad measurement). We do not operate servers that store your personal data.

6. Data deletion

You can request deletion of data at any time. Because the Game has no accounts, the steps are:

  1. Local game data: uninstall the Game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Ground Ace 1944 → Storage → Clear data.
  2. Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads. This unlinks previously collected ad data from your device.
  3. Requests to us: email ourpuppy8373@gmail.com with the subject "Ground Ace 1944 data deletion". We will respond within 30 days and forward the request to our ad partners where applicable.

Full instructions: Data Deletion page.
