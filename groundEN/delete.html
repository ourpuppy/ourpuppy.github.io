Ground Ace 1944 — Data Deletion Request

Developer: OurPuppy · Contact: ourpuppy8373@gmail.com · App package: com.ourpuppy.groundace1944

Ground Ace 1944 does not use accounts. There is no account to delete, and we do not operate servers that store your personal data. The data that exists is (1) game progress on your device and (2) advertising data held by our ad partners. You can delete both as follows.

1. Delete game data stored on your device

  • Uninstall the Game, or
  • Open Android Settings → Apps → Ground Ace 1944 → Storage & cache → Clear storage.

This permanently removes level progress, coins, unlocked items, settings and your ad-consent choice. Purchases made through Google Play (e.g. "Remove Ads") can be restored from the in-game store after reinstalling.

2. Delete or reset advertising data

  • Open Android Settings → Google → Ads (on some devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Tap "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID".

Our ad partners (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads) identify devices only by the advertising ID. Deleting or resetting it unlinks previously collected advertising data from your device. You may also submit a request directly to Unity: Unity Privacy Policy.

3. Send a deletion request to us

Email: ourpuppy8373@gmail.com
Subject: Ground Ace 1944 data deletion
Please include: your device model, the approximate date you played, and (optionally) your Google Play order number if your request concerns a purchase.

We will confirm your request within 30 days, delete any data we hold (such as support emails), and forward the request to our advertising partners where applicable. We do not need to retain any of your data after deletion, except records we must keep by law (for example, purchase records held by Google Play).

What is deleted vs. kept

  • Deleted: all local game data (by you, steps 1), advertising profile linked to your advertising ID (step 2), any support correspondence (step 3).
  • Kept: aggregated, anonymous statistics that cannot identify you; Google Play purchase records managed by Google.
