How to delete your data

App
Zombie Road Rush: Truck Mods (Google Play, package com.ourpuppy.zombietruck)
Developer
OurPuppy — ourpuppy8373@gmail.com
Account
There are no accounts or logins. Progress is stored on your device with a random player ID, so "account deletion" is done by the data deletion below.

Option 1. Delete in the Game

  1. On the title screen, tap ≡ (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Tap Erase Data and confirm with Erase.
  3. All game data on the device (coins, trucks and parts, region records, shelter village, daily missions, Today's Road record) is deleted immediately and the Game starts over.

Option 2. Ask us by email (includes ad and analytics partners)

  1. Email ourpuppy8373@gmail.com with the subject [Zombie Road Rush] Data deletion request.
  2. Include your player ID (bottom of Settings) and device model. If you already uninstalled the Game, include the device model and roughly when you played.
  3. We confirm receipt and, within 30 days, ask our partners (GameAnalytics, Unity LevelPlay/ironSource) to delete records linked to your player ID, then email you the result.
