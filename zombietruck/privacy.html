Privacy Policy

Effective: December 1, 2026 · Version 1

OurPuppy (Korean trade name: 우리강아지, "we") operates the mobile game "Zombie Road Rush: Truck Mods" (package com.ourpuppy.zombietruck, the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game collects and why, who we share it with, and how you can delete it.

Summary
- There is no sign-up, no login and no server of ours. We do not collect your name, phone number or email (unless you email us). Your coins, trucks and village are stored only on your device.
- To keep the Game free we use Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) ads (interstitials after a run, rewarded ads), and GameAnalytics to improve the Game. No ads are shown while you drive.
- Paid items (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, Coin Packs) are bought through Google Play. We never receive your payment details.
- Analytics and personalized ads are optional. We ask on first launch, and you can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy choices.
- Settings > Erase Data deletes your data on the device right away; you can ask us by email to delete partner records.

3. Retention

- Device data: until you use "Erase Data" or uninstall the Game. We keep nothing on a server.
- Ad and analytics partners: per their retention periods; when you ask us, we ask them to delete your records within 30 days.
- Support emails: deleted one year after the issue is closed (longer only while a dispute is ongoing).

4. Your rights

You may access, correct or delete your information, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time.
- Change consent: Settings > Privacy choices
- Delete device data: Settings > Erase Data
- Delete or access partner records: email ourpuppy8373@gmail.com with your player ID (shown at the bottom of Settings)
- Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings > Google > Ads
We respond within 30 days. See Data Deletion. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in Korea; under 16 where local law requires parental consent for data processing). If we learn that a child's data was collected without a parent's consent, we will delete it. Parents can contact us at the email above.

6. Security

We use only SDKs that encrypt data in transit (HTTPS), collect as little as possible, and do not collect directly identifying information in the Game.
