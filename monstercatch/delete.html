How to delete your data

App
Monster Catcher: Survival (Google Play, package com.ourpuppy.monstercatch)
Developer
OurPuppy — ourpuppy8373@gmail.com
Accounts
The app has no user accounts or sign-in. Your progress is stored on your device with a random Player ID, so "account deletion" is done as the data deletion below.

Option 1. Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Monster Catcher and tap Settings (gear icon, bottom right of the lobby).
  2. Tap Delete my data, then Delete.
  3. All game data on the device is erased at once and analytics stops. The confirmation shows your Player ID and a Request by email button for deleting partner records (Option 2).

Option 2. Request deletion by email (includes ad and analytics partner records)

  1. Email ourpuppy8373@gmail.com with the subject [Monster Catcher] Data deletion request.
  2. Include your Player ID (Settings screen, or the confirmation after Option 1) and your device model. If you already uninstalled the app, include your device model and roughly when you played.
  3. We reply to confirm, ask our partners (GameAnalytics, Unity LevelPlay/ironSource) to delete the records linked to your Player ID within 30 days, and email you the result.
