Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026 · Version 1

OurPuppy ("we", "us") operates the mobile game "Monster Catcher" (Korean title 「몬스터 잡아 키우기: 포획 서바이벌」, Japanese title 「モンスターキャッチャー：捕獲サバイバル」, package com.ourpuppy.monstercatch, the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game collects, why, who we share it with, and how you can delete it.

Summary
- There is no account sign-up. We do not collect your name, phone number or email (except when you email us).
- We use Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) to show ads that keep the Game free, and GameAnalytics to improve the Game.
- Personalized ads and analytics are optional. Change them anytime in Settings > Privacy settings.
- Settings > Delete my data erases your data on the device immediately; you can request deletion of partner records by email.

3. Retention

- On-device data: until you use "Delete my data" or uninstall the Game.
- Partner data: per each partner's retention policy; when you ask us to delete it, we forward the request to the partners within 30 days.
- Support emails: deleted one year after the request is resolved (longer only if a dispute is ongoing).
- Transaction records required by law are kept for the legally required period; payments are handled by Google Play and we never store payment details.

4. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Korea's PIPA or Japan's APPI) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.
- Change or withdraw consent: Settings > Privacy settings in the Game (turning off personalized ads also signals "do not sell or share").
- Delete on-device data: Settings > Delete my data.
- Access or delete partner records: email ourpuppy8373@gmail.com with your Player ID (shown in Settings).
- Reset your advertising ID / opt out of personalized ads: Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
We respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. See Data Deletion for details.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 14 (or a higher age where local law requires). We ask users to confirm their age on first launch. If we learn that we collected data from a child without valid parental consent, we will delete it. Parents can contact us at the email below.

6. Security

We only use SDKs that encrypt data in transit (HTTPS), keep collection to a minimum, and do not collect directly identifying information in the Game.

7. Changes

We will announce changes in the Game or on this page at least 7 days before they take effect (30 days for changes that reduce your rights), and ask for consent again on the first screen when required.
