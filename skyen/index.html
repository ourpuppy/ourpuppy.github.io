OurPuppy (representative: Jeongeun Choi; "we", "us") develops and publishes the mobile game Sky Ace 1944 (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play the Game, how it is used, who receives it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android version distributed through Google Play.

Summary
  • The Game has no account or sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or other information that identifies you directly.
  • Your progress (stages, coins, medals, settings) is stored only on your device. We do not operate servers that store your data.
  • Advertising (Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads), game analytics (GameAnalytics) and in-app purchases (Google Play Billing) automatically collect device and advertising identifiers and related technical data through their SDKs.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings at any time. Users in the EU/EEA and the UK are asked for consent on first launch.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the Game and save your progress
  • To show ads (interstitial ads between missions and rewarded ads you choose to watch) and grant ad rewards
  • To deliver, verify and restore in-app purchases
  • To analyze aggregated usage and errors so we can improve the Game
  • To comply with law and prevent abuse

3. Legal Bases (EU/EEA and UK users)

  • Consent – personalized advertising and analytics identifiers. You give or refuse consent in the dialog shown on first launch and can change it in the Game's settings.
  • Performance of a contract – delivering the Game and your in-app purchases.
  • Legitimate interests – non-personalized ads, fraud prevention, security and aggregated statistics needed to operate the Game.

4. Retention

  • On-device data is kept until you uninstall the Game or clear its app data, and is deleted immediately at that point.
  • Data collected by third-party services is kept according to their own policies. We do not store a copy of it.
    • Unity: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy and unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
    • GameAnalytics: gameanalytics.com/privacy
    • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Support emails you send us are kept for up to 3 years after the request is resolved, then deleted.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Opt out of personalized ads: Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization". You will then see non-personalized ads.
  • EU/EEA and UK users (GDPR / UK GDPR): you may accept or refuse consent in the dialog shown on first launch and change your choice at any time in the Game's settings. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
  • California and other US state residents (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws): use the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" option in the Game's settings to stop sharing for advertising. We do not sell personal information for money.
  • Delete on-device data: uninstall the Game or clear its app data in Android settings. All progress is removed.
  • Access or delete data held by our providers: email us with your advertising identifier and we will forward the request or explain how to contact the provider directly. Unity also offers an opt-out in its privacy policy.

How to request deletion of your data

  • Ask us: email ourpuppy8373@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and your device's Advertising ID (Android Settings → Google → Ads). We forward the request to the providers below within 10 days and confirm the result. We keep no personal data on servers of our own, so there is nothing further for us to delete.
  • Ask Unity (LevelPlay / Unity Ads) directly: Unity accepts deletion requests for advertising data. Use the data-rights process in unity.com/legal/privacy-policy or email DPO@unity3d.com with your Advertising ID.
  • Ask GameAnalytics directly: email privacy@gameanalytics.com with the game name (Sky Ace 1944) and your Advertising ID or device identifier, and your analytics data will be deleted.
  • On-device data: uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data deletes it immediately.

7. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older (14 and older in the Republic of Korea). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Identifiers and Similar Technologies

The Game does not use cookies. Advertising and analytics SDKs use the device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and a random per-install identifier. You can reset or delete the advertising identifier in your device settings.

9. Security

  • We do not operate servers that store your personal information.
  • Save data lives only on your device and is protected against tampering with a checksum.
  • Communication with third-party services is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Our providers protect data under their own security programs.
