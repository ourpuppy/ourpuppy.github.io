How to delete your data

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Developer: OurPuppy · ourpuppy8373@gmail.com

App
Animal Painters Idle: Color Mix Defense (Google Play, package com.ourpuppy.paintmage)
Developer

The Game has no account, no login and no server of ours. Your progress is stored only on your device, so there is no account to delete: the steps below remove everything.

1. Data on your device

  1. Open Animal Painters Idle and open Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my data and confirm. The save, your settings and your consent choices are erased at once.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Animal Painters Idle > Storage, does the same.

2. Records held by our analytics and ad providers

  1. Before deleting your data, copy the Player ID shown in Settings in the Game (tap it to copy). It is erased together with your data, and without it we cannot find your records.
  2. Email ourpuppy8373@gmail.com with the subject [Animal Painters Idle] Data deletion request and your Player ID.
  3. We ask GameAnalytics to delete the analytics records linked to that Player ID and email you the result.

Advertising records are held by the ad companies under your device's advertising ID. You can reset or delete that ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads, or use each company's own controls:

- Unity (ironSource LevelPlay, Unity Ads, ironSource Ads): privacy policy and data requests
- Google AdMob: privacy policy, My Ad Center
- GameAnalytics: privacy notice and opt-out

Please note. Deleting your data, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently removes your progress and all virtual items, including unused gems. We have no copy, cannot restore them, and they are not refunded. Only the permanent purchases (Starter Pack, Remove Ads, Golden Palette) can be restored, with the same Google account, through Shop > Restore purchases.
