3. Retention and deletion

- On-device data: until you use "Delete my data", clear the app's storage or uninstall the Game.

- Records that the law requires us to keep: for the legally required period.
After that, the data is deleted so that it cannot be recovered.

4. Your rights and choices

Depending on the law that applies to you (for example the GDPR, the CCPA, Korea's PIPA or Japan's APPI), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to withdraw consent, to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for targeted advertising, and to complain to your data protection authority.
- Consent: the two switches in Settings in the Game. Turning off "Show ads tailored to me" also opts you out of the sale or sharing of your data for targeted advertising.
- Deleting your data: see Data Deletion.
- Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
- Other requests: email ourpuppy8373@gmail.com with the Player ID shown in Settings. We respond within the period required by law.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in the Republic of Korea, or a higher age where your country requires it), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

6. Security

We keep what we handle to a minimum: the Game collects no directly identifying information and we run no server. Support emails can be accessed only by the person in charge.

7. Changes

We post changes to this policy on this page before they take effect. Where the law requires your consent to a change, we ask for it in the Game.
