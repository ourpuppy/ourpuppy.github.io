Summon Odds (Painter Summon)

As of November 12, 2026 · same values as "Rates" on the Painter Summon screen in the Game · Developer: OurPuppy · ourpuppy8373@gmail.com

The Painter Summon is a random item. Each summon gives one painter: first the grade is decided at the rates below, then one painter of that grade is chosen with equal chance. Every summon — including each one of a x10 summon and free summons from ads — uses the same odds, regardless of earlier results.

Duplicates

A painter you already have gains 1 star instead, up to 5 stars. A painter who is already at 5 stars is returned as gems: Common 10, Rare 25, Epic 60.

Picture Cards (boss treasure chest)

After a boss, the treasure chest offers 3 picture cards and you choose one. The 3 cards are drawn one at a time without repeats, at the rates below. "Draw again" (paid with gems) draws 3 new cards at the same rates. The card you are currently using is not offered; the rates of the other cards rise in proportion.
