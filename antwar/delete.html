How to delete your data

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Developer: OurPuppy · ourpuppy8373@gmail.com

App
Real Ant War: Colony Simulator (Google Play, package com.ourpuppy.antwar)
Developer
OurPuppy — ourpuppy8373@gmail.com

The Game has no account, no login and no server of ours. Your progress is stored only on your device, so there is no account to delete: the steps below remove everything.

1. Data on your device

  1. Open Real Ant War, tap Play to enter your colony, and open Settings (the gear button).
  2. Tap Start over and confirm. Your progress, your privacy choice and your Player ID are erased at once.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Real Ant War > Storage, does the same.

2. Records held by our analytics and ad providers

  1. Before deleting your data, copy the Player ID shown in Settings in the Game (tap it to copy). It is erased together with your data, and without it we cannot find your records.
  2. Email ourpuppy8373@gmail.com with the subject [Real Ant War] Data deletion request and your Player ID.
  3. We ask GameAnalytics to delete the analytics records linked to that Player ID and email you the result.

Advertising records are held by the ad companies under your device's advertising ID. You can reset or delete that ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads, or use each company's own controls:

- Unity (ironSource LevelPlay, Unity Ads, ironSource Ads): privacy policy and data requests
- Google AdMob: privacy policy, My Ad Center
- GameAnalytics: privacy notice and opt-out

Please note. Deleting your data, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently removes your progress and all virtual items. We have no copy, cannot restore them, and they are not refunded. The paid products (Premium, Starter Pack, Queen Pack, Night Shift, Everything Pack) are tied to your Google account, not to this data: they stay yours, and after a reinstall you can get them back through Shop > Restore purchase.
