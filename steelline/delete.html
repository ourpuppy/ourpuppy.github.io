데이터 삭제 안내

개발사: OurPuppy · 앱: 강철 방어선: 탱크 디펜스 (com.ourpuppy.steelline)

게임에는 회원 계정이 없으므로 「계정 삭제」는 아래의 「데이터 삭제」로 대신합니다.

방법 1. 게임 안에서 바로 삭제

1) 게임 실행 → 아래 탭 설정
2) 내 데이터 삭제 → 삭제
3) 기기 안의 진행도·재화·부대·설정·동의 기록이 즉시 영구 삭제되고, 분석 데이터 전송이 멈춥니다. 게임은 처음 화면부터 다시 시작합니다.

방법 2. 메일로 삭제 요청 (광고·분석 협력사 기록 포함)

받는 사람: ourpuppy8373@gmail.com
제목: [강철 방어선] 데이터 삭제 요청
내용: 플레이어 ID(게임 설정 화면 아래에 표시, 데이터 삭제 직후 안내 창에도 표시), 기기 모델
게임 안 「삭제 요청 메일 보내기」 버튼을 누르면 위 내용이 자동으로 채워집니다.

Data Deletion

Developer: OurPuppy · App: Steel Line: WW2 Tank Defense (com.ourpuppy.steelline)

The Game has no user accounts, so "account deletion" is handled as the data deletion described below.

Option 1. Delete in the Game

1) Open the Game → Settings tab
2) Delete My Data → Delete
3) Progress, currencies, squads, settings and consent records on your device are permanently deleted at once, and analytics stops. The Game restarts from the first screen.

Option 2. Request deletion by email (includes partner records)

To: ourpuppy8373@gmail.com
Subject: [Steel Line] Data deletion request
Include your Player ID (shown at the bottom of Settings and in the confirmation after deleting) and your device model.
The in-game "Send Deletion Request" button fills this in for you.

What is deleted and when

DataActionTiming
On-device game data (progress, currencies, settings, consent record, Player ID)DeletedImmediately
GameAnalytics records (by Player ID)Deletion requested from the partnerWithin 30 days
Advertising records (Unity LevelPlay/ironSource and ad networks, by advertising ID)Deletion requested from partners; we also recommend deleting/resetting your advertising ID in Android settingsWithin 30 days
Support emailsDeletedWithin 1 year after resolution
Purchase recordsKept by Google Play as required by law (we never store payment details)As required by law
Non-consumable purchases such as Remove Ads are recorded by Google Play and can be restored with the same Google account after deletion. Consumed currencies such as gems cannot be restored.
