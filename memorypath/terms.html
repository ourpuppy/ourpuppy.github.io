2. The service

The Game is free to download and is supported by ads (rewarded and interstitial) and optional in-app purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, coin packs, Snow Stage Pack, Power Potions). "Remove Ads" removes only the interstitial ads shown between stages; rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for items, double rewards, a continue, a duel retry, an extra treasure-hunt try or the daily chest) remain available. Clearing stages builds up "Power" (energy), and every fifth stage is a duel against a monster in the arena. Losing a duel does not send you back to the start; you only lose Power, which can be refilled with Power Potions (a paid product or an in-game reward). Winning a duel grants coins in proportion to your remaining Power. A duel you have already won cannot be replayed (tapping it on the map only shows a notice, and "Next" skips it); a lost duel can be retried.
When you first clear a stage marked with a treasure chest on the map, a "Treasure Hunt" bonus stage follows: you memorize where the chests are, then find them under the fog, and receive coins and Power for each chest found. The treasure hunt is available only once per stage; it is recorded as completed the moment it starts, so leaving it early or clearing that stage again does not reopen it. Chest positions are randomized each time.
When you replay a stage you have already cleared, the coin reward is paid in full only if you earn more stars than before; otherwise 30% of the reward is paid (a notice is shown before you start). This rule does not apply to the daily challenge, treasure hunts or duels.
Your progress is stored on your device; there is no game server and no account.
