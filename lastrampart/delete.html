Data Deletion

Game: Last Rampart: Castle Defense · Developer: OurPuppy · Package: com.ourpuppy.lastrampart · Effective: October 21, 2026

The game has no accounts and never asks for your name or contact details. Game data is stored only on your device, and you can delete it at any time.

1. Delete inside the game

  1. Open the game and tap Settings (gear) at the top right of the lobby.
  2. Tap Delete My Data and confirm.
  3. Your conquest progress, coins, upgrades, settings, consent record and purchase records are erased from the device and the game starts fresh.

"Remove Ads" and the "Starter Pack" stay on your Google Play account, so you can get them back with Restore in the shop. Coins already delivered cannot be restored.

2. Uninstall

Uninstalling the game removes all game data stored on the device.
