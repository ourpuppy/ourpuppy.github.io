2. How we use it

Only to improve difficulty and features, find errors, and show ads at a reasonable frequency. We do not sell your information.

3. Consent and choices

Analytics and ads start only after you agree on the first-launch consent screen. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Uninstalling the game removes all game data stored on the device.

4. Retention

Data collected by third-party services is kept according to their policies. We do not keep any personal data ourselves.

5. Children

This game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
