How to request deletion

  1. In the Game, tap Settings → Delete My Data. All game data and consent records on your device are deleted immediately.
  2. If the Game is not installed or you prefer to ask outside the app, email ourpuppy8373@gmail.com with your Player ID (shown at the bottom of Settings). We process requests within 30 days.
  3. Uninstalling the app also deletes the data stored on your device.
