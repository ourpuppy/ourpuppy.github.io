Privacy Policy

Developer: OurPuppy · Contact: ourpuppy8373@gmail.com · Effective date: October 21, 2026 · App package: com.ourpuppy.sniperfront

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Sniper Front 1944 (the "Game"), published by OurPuppy ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The Game is a free, single-player sniper game for Android supported by advertising and optional in-app purchases.

1. Summary

  • The Game does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Game progress (missions, stars, gold, weapon upgrades, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • Our advertising partner (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource) may collect your device's advertising ID and technical device data to show and measure ads.
  • Our analytics service (GameAnalytics) collects anonymous gameplay statistics (sessions, mission progress, ad and purchase events) to improve the Game. Nothing is sent if you decline.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details.
  • You are asked for consent on first launch and can change your choice at any time from the Privacy button in the main menu. See Data Deletion for how to delete data.

4. Advertising, analytics and your choices

On first launch the Game shows a consent dialog.

  • Agree: personalized ads may be shown using your advertising ID, and anonymous gameplay statistics are sent to GameAnalytics.
  • Decline: ads are still shown, but we pass a "no consent" signal to our ad partners so your data is not used for personalized advertising, and analytics submission is turned off. Game features are identical either way.

You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy button in the main menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Purchasing "Remove Ads" disables interstitial ads; rewarded ads remain available only when you choose to watch them.

5. Data retention

Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its storage. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies (typically up to 13 months for ad measurement). GameAnalytics retains event data for aggregated statistics and does not use it to identify individual players. We do not operate servers that store your personal data.

6. Data deletion

You can request deletion at any time. Because the Game has no accounts, the steps are:

  1. In the Game: Menu → Privacy → Delete my data on this device. Progress is erased and analytics submission is turned off.
  2. Local game data: uninstall the Game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Sniper Front 1944 → Storage → Clear data.
  3. Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  4. Request to us: email ourpuppy8373@gmail.com with the subject "Sniper Front 1944 data deletion". We respond within 30 days and forward the request to our ad and analytics partners where applicable.

Full instructions: Data Deletion page
