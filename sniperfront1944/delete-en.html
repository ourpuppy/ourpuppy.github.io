Data Deletion Request

App: Sniper Front 1944 · Developer: OurPuppy · Contact: ourpuppy8373@gmail.com · App package: com.ourpuppy.sniperfront

Sniper Front 1944 does not use accounts. There is no account to delete, and we do not operate servers that store your personal data. The data that exists is (1) game progress on your device, (2) advertising data held by our ad partner, and (3) anonymous gameplay statistics held by our analytics service. You can delete all of it, or stop it being sent, as follows.

1. Delete from inside the Game (easiest)

Main menu → Privacy → Delete my data on this device → Confirm

Mission progress, stars, gold, unlocked weapons and settings are erased from the device, and analytics submission and personalized-ads consent are set to "declined". Purchases made through Google Play (e.g. "Remove Ads") can be restored from the in-game store with Restore purchases.

2. Delete game data stored on your device

  • Uninstall the Game, or
  • Open Android Settings → Apps → Sniper Front 1944 → Storage & cache → Clear storage.

3. Delete or reset advertising data

  • Open Android Settings → Google → Ads (on some devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Tap "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID".

Our ad partners (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads) identify devices only by the advertising ID. Deleting or resetting it unlinks previously collected advertising data from your device. You may also submit a request directly to Unity: Unity Privacy Policy.

4. Gameplay analytics data

GameAnalytics holds only anonymous statistics keyed by a randomly generated ID. Choosing "Decline" in the in-game Privacy menu stops further submission. To delete data already sent, email us (below) and we will forward the request to GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics Privacy Policy).

5. Send a deletion request to us

Email: ourpuppy8373@gmail.com
Subject: Sniper Front 1944 data deletion
Please include: your device model, the approximate date you played, and (optionally) your Google Play order number if your request concerns a purchase.

We confirm your request within 30 days, delete any data we hold (such as support emails), and forward the request to our advertising and analytics partners where applicable.
