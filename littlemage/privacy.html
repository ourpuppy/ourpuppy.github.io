Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Version 1

OurPuppy ("we", "us") operates the mobile game "Idle Mage: Wizard RPG" (Korean title 「마법사 키우기 : 방치형 RPG」, Japanese title 「魔法使い育成：放置RPG」, package com.ourpuppy.littlemage, the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game collects, why, who processes it or receives it, and how you can delete it.

Summary
- There is no account sign-up or login. We do not collect your name, phone number or email (except when you email us).
- Your save data is stored only on your device. We do not run our own server that stores player data.
- We use Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) for rewarded video ads that keep the Game free, GameAnalytics to improve the Game, and Google Play Billing for purchases.
- Personalized ads and analytics are optional. Change them anytime in Settings > Privacy Settings.
- Settings > Delete My Data erases your data on the device immediately; you can request deletion of partner records by email.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only as follows:
- If you allow personalized ads, ad networks connected through LevelPlay mediation may use your advertising ID and ad interaction data for interest-based advertising. If you turn this off, we send LevelPlay a non-consent signal and a "do not sell" signal for later ad requests.
- When required by law or valid legal process.
- Payments are a contract between you and Google; Google's privacy policy applies to the payment data Google collects.

5. Personalized ads and your choices

With your consent, LevelPlay and its connected ad networks use your advertising ID and ad interaction data (impressions, views, clicks, app usage) to show ads that match your interests. You can turn this off in Settings > Privacy Settings, and you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads). The Game is configured as not directed to children in the ad SDK.

6. Retention

- On-device data: until you use "Delete My Data" or uninstall the Game.
- Partner data: per each partner's retention policy; when you ask us to delete it, we forward the request to the partners within 10 days.
- Support emails: deleted one year after the request is resolved (longer only if a dispute is ongoing).
- Transaction records required by law are kept for the legally required period; payments are handled by Google Play and we never store payment details.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Korea's PIPA or Japan's APPI) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing), and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
- Change or withdraw consent: Settings > Privacy Settings in the Game (turning off personalized ads also sends a "do not sell or share" signal).
- Delete on-device data: Settings > Delete My Data.
- Access or delete partner records: email ourpuppy8373@gmail.com with your Player ID (shown at the bottom of Settings). We hold no names, so we use the Player ID to find your records.
- Reset your advertising ID / opt out of personalized ads: Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
We act on requests within 10 days and report the result, including the partners' response, within 30 days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. See Data Deletion for details.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 14 (or a higher age where local law requires). We ask users to confirm they are 14 or older on first launch. If we learn that we collected data from a child without valid parental consent, we will delete it. Parents can contact us at the email below.

9. Security

We collect as little as possible, do not collect directly identifying information in the Game, do not run our own player-data server, only use SDKs that encrypt data in transit (HTTPS), and do not start the ad or analytics SDKs before consent. Only the operator handles support emails and partner dashboards.

10. Changes

We will announce changes in the Game or on this page at least 7 days before they take effect (30 days for changes that reduce your rights), and ask for consent again on the first screen when required. This is version 1, first effective on October 27, 2026.
