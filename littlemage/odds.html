Odds Disclosure

As of October 27, 2026 · Applies from the launch version

The random items in this Game are spell summons and packs that contain a random spell. For every summon, ① a grade is chosen with the odds below, then ② one of the 6 spells (Fireball, Meteor, Ice Spear, Blizzard, Lightning, Chain Lightning) is chosen with equal odds (1/6 each). Every summon is independent: the odds never change based on earlier results, and there is no pity or guaranteed result after a set number of summons. The odds are the same whether you use purchased or free gems, a summon ticket, or a free ad summon. The same grade table is shown under "View Odds" on the Spells screen.

Merging (no randomness)

Merging 3 copies of the same spell at the same grade always gives 1 copy of that spell one grade higher. Merging always succeeds and involves no odds.

Percentages are rounded to four decimal places (exact value = grade odds ÷ 6). We announce changes to odds or to summon and pack contents in the Game and on this page before they apply.
