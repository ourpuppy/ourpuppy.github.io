Privacy Policy

Effective: December 1, 2026 · Version 1

OurPuppy (Korean trade name 우리강아지, "we") respects the privacy of everyone who plays the mobile game "Upstairs Neighbor: Escape" (Korean title 층간소음: 윗집 탈출, package name com.ourpuppy.upstairs, the "Game") and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what information the Game collects and why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

Summary
- No account, no login, no company server. We do not collect names, phone numbers or email addresses (except when you email us). Game records such as endings, achievements, collected drawings and settings are stored only on your device.
- To keep the Game free we use Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) ads (an interstitial on the result screen after you are caught, and rewarded ads you choose to watch), and GameAnalytics to improve the Game.
- There are only two paid products, "Remove Interstitial Ads" and "VIP Package"; both are one-time purchases that stay with you, bought through Google Play billing. The Game has no virtual currency such as gems. We never receive payment details.
- Usage statistics and personalized ads are optional. We ask on first launch, and you can change your choice anytime in Settings > Change Stats & Ad Consent.
- Settings > Delete My Data deletes everything on the device immediately; partner records can be deleted by email.

3. Retention

- Device data: until you use "Erase my data" or uninstall the app. Nothing is stored on a company server.
- Partner data: per each partner's retention; when you request deletion we ask the partners within 30 days.
- Support emails: deleted one year after the matter is closed (or when a dispute is resolved).
- Transaction records required by law are kept for the statutory period (for example 5 years for payment and supply records under the Korean E-Commerce Act; Google Play processes payments and we keep no payment details).

4. Deletion

Data whose retention period has ended or whose deletion was requested is destroyed without delay in a non-recoverable way; partner-held data is deleted through each partner's deletion process (deletion API or request form).

5. Your rights

You may access, correct or delete your data, restrict processing, or withdraw consent at any time.
- Change consent: Settings > Change Stats & Ad Consent
- Delete device data: Settings > Delete My Data
- Delete or access partner records: ourpuppy8373@gmail.com (include your device model and the approximate period you played)
- Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads: Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads)
We respond within 30 days. See Data Deletion for details. Residents of the EU/UK may lodge a complaint with their supervisory authority. California residents have the right to know, delete and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; we do not sell personal information, and declining personalized ads opts you out of sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Children

The Game is a horror game intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 14 (or the age required in your country, e.g. 13 in the USA and 16 in parts of the EU). The Game has no way to enter directly identifying information. If we learn that a child's data was collected without parental consent we delete it promptly; parents may contact us at the address above.

7. Automatic collection

The Game does not use web cookies. Ad and analytics SDKs use the advertising ID and device identifiers, which you can reset or delete in Android settings.

8. Security

We use only SDKs that encrypt data in transit (HTTPS), collect the minimum necessary, and never collect directly identifying data such as names or contact details inside the Game.
