How to delete your data

App
Upstairs Neighbor: Escape (Google Play, package com.ourpuppy.upstairs)
Developer
OurPuppy — ourpuppy8373@gmail.com
Account
There is no account, login or company server, so there is no account to delete. Progress is stored only on your device together with a random player ID; "account deletion" therefore means the data deletion described below.

Option 1. Delete inside the game

  1. Open the game and tap Settings at the top right of the title screen.
  2. Tap Erase my data and confirm with OK.
  3. All game data on the device (endings, achievements and collected drawings, today's best time and escape streak, difficulty, settings (sound, vibration, sensitivity, left-handed mode, language, notifications), consent status, purchase status, tutorial completion, ad display count, player ID) is erased immediately and analytics stop. The game returns to its first screen.

Option 2. Request by email (including ad and analytics partner records)

  1. Email ourpuppy8373@gmail.com with the subject [Upstairs Neighbor] Data deletion request.
  2. Include your device model and the approximate period you played. You can send the request even if you have already uninstalled the app.
  3. We confirm receipt, ask our partners (GameAnalytics, Unity LevelPlay/ironSource) to delete records linked to your device and player ID within 30 days, and email you the result.
